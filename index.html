Automated (AI-driven) press operator instructions

Growing

Translating printing and job planning experience into logical commands, so AI can help press operators in colour settings and paper format grouping without reaching for expensive proprietary tools or software. Just HTML files that run on any browser. Accessible, low-cost approach. No extra fees, no monthly subscriptions.

  • Starts from raw Excel data, no new software required
  • Makes that data visible at a glance
  • Generates structured, clear handouts for operators
  • Drag and drop job colours onto the printing units, swap colours easily
  • Save fixed colour sequences so they fill in automatically
  • Colour swatches added to improve visibility (imported from colour library cxf-file)
  • Highlighted specifications where they matter
  • Clear operator instructions that reduce the chance of errors
  • Works with both CMYK and Extended Colour Gamut (ECG) printing
  • Automatically groups jobs by paper size, paper type, and die form
  • Calculates the minimal number of colour changes (washups) needed
  • Manual override always possible
  • Aimed at increasing press efficiency
